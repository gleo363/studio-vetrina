# Studio Vetrina — Brand Foundation

*The single source of truth for the whole project: pitch deck, logo, brand guidelines, website, and app. Brand-facing copy is in Italian; explanations are in English.*

---

## 1. The concept in one line

**La tua vetrina sul mondo.**

A Rome web studio that gives small businesses an online presence as beautiful and well-tended as their shop window. In Italy the *vetrina* (shop window) is sacred — the florist, the baker, the boutique all dress it with care. The internet is the new street, and Studio Vetrina are the artisans who dress your window there.

---

## 2. Name

- **Formal name:** Studio Vetrina
- **Short form:** Vetrina
- **Wordmark:** lowercase `vetrina` (warm, modern, approachable)
- **Why "Studio":** reads as a design atelier in Italian, signals craft over tech, and is more ownable/searchable than the bare word.

> *Note: worth checking domain (.it / .com) and trademark availability before final lock — I can run that check whenever you want.*

---

## 3. Positioning

- **Who it's for:** piccole attività e artigiani romani — il ristoratore, la fiorista, il parrucchiere, lo studio dentistico, la boutique, l'idraulico. People proud of their work but alienated by "tech."
- **The promise (la promessa):** *Curiamo la tua presenza online come tu curi la tua vetrina.*
- **The enemy:** the cold, jargon-soaked, English-heavy agency that talks down to small business owners. Vetrina speaks human Italian.
- **Category:** studio di web design — siti su misura per piccole attività.
- **Manifesto line (great for the deck):** *Crediamo che ogni attività, anche la più piccola, meriti di essere vista.*

---

## 4. Brand personality

Five traits to keep every decision consistent:

- **Accogliente** — welcoming, never intimidating
- **Curato** — meticulous, well-tended, every detail dressed
- **Chiaro** — clear, plain-spoken, zero jargon
- **Orgoglioso del mestiere** — proud of the craft
- **Romano** — rooted, warm, with a little character

---

## 5. Verbal identity (voice & tone)

### The signature rule: *Italiano, sempre*
The brand's biggest differentiator. Most agencies pepper everything with English. Vetrina deliberately uses clear Italian — that *is* the modern part. A working lexicon:

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Use |
|---|---|
| website | sito |
| landing page | pagina (di benvenuto) |
| responsive | si adatta a ogni schermo |
| custom | su misura |
| performance | velocità |
| booking | prenotazioni |
| template | modello |
| deadline | consegna |
| meeting | incontro / due chiacchiere |
| feedback | pareri / cosa ne pensi |

### Address: **"tu"**
Use informal *tu* — friendly, modern, close. (*Lei* is the traditional fallback if a specific client prefers formality.)

### Tone
Warm, clear, confident, lightly playful. Short sentences. Concrete, never abstract.

### Tagline
- **Primary:** *La tua vetrina sul mondo.*
- **Rotational:** *Bella anche online.* / *Ogni attività merita la sua vetrina.* / *Mettiamo in mostra il tuo lavoro.*

### Boilerplate — *Chi siamo*
> Studio Vetrina è uno studio di web design di Roma. Curiamo la presenza online delle piccole attività con la stessa attenzione con cui un negoziante cura la sua vetrina: con gusto, ordine e un po' di orgoglio. Niente paroloni, niente inglese inutile — solo siti su misura, belli e facili da usare.

### Sample microcopy
- **Bottone (CTA):** *Apriamo la tua vetrina* · *Parliamone davanti a un caffè*
- **Inizio modulo:** *Raccontaci la tua attività — al resto pensiamo noi.*
- **Pagina 404:** *Questa vetrina è vuota. Torniamo alla home?*

## 6. Visual direction

*(The brief for the logo and guidelines — to be executed next.)*

### Logo concept — *la cornice*
A **window within a window**: the wordmark `vetrina` paired with a mark that's a rounded-corner rectangle containing a smaller offset rectangle — a shop window, a screen, a framed view. This frame becomes the universal container device across everything.

### Color — *Palette Romana*
Grounded specifically in Rome, not generic Italy.

| Name | Role | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Travertino | background / warm off-white | `#F3EEE4` |
| Inchiostro | text / near-black | `#1B1A18` |
| Cotto | primary accent (Pompeian terracotta) | `#BF4D2C` |
| Ocra | secondary accent (Roman ochre) | `#DA9A47` |
| Pietra | neutral UI grey | `#8A8578` |

### Typography
- **Headings / wordmark:** *Fraunces* — a modern serif with old-style warmth; editorial and Italian-elegant.
- **Body / UI:** *Inter* — clean, neutral, highly legible.
- *(All-sans alternative for a cooler, more modernist feel: Archivo + Inter.)*
- Both are free and web-ready, so they carry straight into the site and app.

### Graphic system
Everything sits inside elegant frames/apertures — content "on display," like a dressed window. Rounded corners echo the logomark.

### Photography
Real Roman small businesses in warm natural light — hands at work, the texture of craft — paired with clean device mockups of the finished sites, shown *inside* frames.

### Motion (site/app)
Content reveals "into the window": gentle slide-and-frame reveals.

## 7. The through-line across deliverables

- **Pitch deck:** opens on the shop-window insight; the manifesto line as the hook.
- **Logo:** the window-in-window mark + lowercase wordmark.
- **Brand guidelines:** this foundation, expanded with the finished logo, color swatches, and type specimens.
- **Website:** Travertino canvas, framed sections, Italian-first copy, *Apriamo la tua vetrina* as the main CTA.
- **App:** the frame system as the UI container; warm Roman palette in light and dark.

*Un solo concetto, una sola voce — dalla prima slide all'ultima schermata.*
